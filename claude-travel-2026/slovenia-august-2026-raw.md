### Important: Weak-swimmer booking rule
For Fratarica or rafting, get a route-specific written yes that the expected water level requires no independent swim, mandatory jump/submersion, underwater obstacle, capsize drill or unassisted raft re-entry. A lifejacket alone is not approval. Otherwise use the listed dry climb or e-bike.

### Important: Protect the weather reserve
Friday 21 August is intentionally uncommitted. Do not consume it with a non-refundable activity: it protects the Triglav summit and the paragliding backup.

### Important: Fitness is part of the booking
Both travellers need the ability to walk 6–10 hours with exposed protected climbing for Triglav. Share swimming ability, weight, medication, cardiac, spinal, joint, claustrophobia or pregnancy status privately with operators. Hydrospeed is excluded because it requires strong moving-water confidence.

### Important: The guide can cancel the summit
Weather, lightning, heat, rockfall and individual condition can all change the route or create a turnaround. Never attempt Triglav’s protected summit section without the confirmed guide because a commercial booking was cancelled.

### Important: GHIC / EHIC is not adventure insurance
Your policy must explicitly cover the named sports, mountain rescue, helicopter evacuation and repatriation. Check altitude limits and exclusions line by line.
Links: [Travel insurance guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/foreign-travel-insurance)
