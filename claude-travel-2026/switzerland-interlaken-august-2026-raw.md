Plain-text complete itinerary for Claude.

Shared constraints:
- Dates: 12–22 August 2026
- Travellers: two friends departing London
- Budget ceiling: £5,000 per person for accommodation, ground transport, activities, meals, specialist insurance and contingency

## Packing and safety

### Air + exposure
- Physical photo ID for skydive
- Closed athletic shoes
- Long trousers and warm synthetic layer
- Thin via-ferrata gloves
- Ankle-supporting hiking boots
- Waterproof shell and trousers

### Mountain daypack
- 20–25L backpack with rain cover
- 1.5–2L refillable bottle
- Sunglasses, SPF50, lip balm and cap
- Picnic food and high-energy snacks
- Blister kit, medication and power bank
- Offline SBB and operator vouchers

### Only if canyon is approved
- Swimsuit and quick-dry towel
- Dry underwear and spare layer
- Written operator acceptance saved offline
- No personal technical gear needed; operator supplies wetsuit, vest, shoes, helmet and harness

### Important: Written approval beats assumptions
Email: ‘One participant is a weak swimmer. Please confirm whether every unavoidable pool crossing is safe with the supplied swim vest and direct guide assistance, and whether all jumps, slides and rope-swing water entries can be bypassed.’ Book only after an unambiguous yes.

### Important: Advanced water is excluded
Lütschine Class III–IV rafting and Chli Schliere canyoning explicitly demand strong swimming and are not itinerary alternatives. Do not book them for this trip.
Links: [Rafting requirements](https://www.outdoor.ch/en/outdoor-adventure/river-rafting-luetschine/) · [Chli requirements](https://www.outdoor.ch/en/outdoor-adventure/canyoning-chli-schliere/)

### Important: The budget is intentionally tight
At £4,972 pp, do not add media packages or new paid fallback activities. If Bernerhof breaches CHF3,100, change hotel; do not consume contingency. Any operator discount is upside, not assumed.

### Important: Guides own the mountain decision
Wet rock, lightning, wind and personal condition can cancel or change both via ferratas. Never self-attempt a guide-cancelled exposed route.
