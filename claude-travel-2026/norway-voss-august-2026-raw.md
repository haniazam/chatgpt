# Norway / Voss Air, Rock & Fjord

Plain-text complete itinerary for Claude.

Shared constraints:
- Dates: 12–22 August 2026
- Travellers: two friends departing London
- Budget ceiling: £5,000 per person for accommodation, ground transport, activities, meals, specialist insurance and contingency
- Flights: shown for planning but excluded from the £5,000 ground-cost ceiling
- Swimming: one traveller is a weak swimmer; water activities require written, route-specific operator approval and every plan includes a dry alternative
- Transport: no rental car; the itinerary minimizes hotel changes and inefficient commuting

**OPTION 04 · WILDEST SCENERY**

A Voss-centred hit list of 4,000m freefall, waterfall abseils, paragliding, rock climbing, e-MTB, a 1,381m fjord zipline, high ropes and indoor flight—with water days conditional for a weak swimmer.

- Dates: WED 12 — SAT 22 AUG 2026
- Route: LONDON → BERGEN → VOSS → BERGEN → LONDON
- Ground cost: ≈£4,400 GROUND · FLIGHTS EXTRA
- Bases: VOSS + AIRPORT BOOKENDS
- Pace: 8.5 / 10 · AIR + FJORD
- Why this one: BIGGEST LANDSCAPE PAYOFF

> **Voss beats Loen for this ten-night window:** Loen would add roughly 13 hours of intercity travel and remove the two air-weather reserves. Keep eight nights in Voss; do the high-value Flåm loop as the only day trip.

## Packing and safety

### Norwegian weather
- Waterproof hooded shell and trousers
- Two merino / synthetic base layers
- Fleece or compact midlayer
- Quick-dry hiking trousers
- Grippy hiking shoes plus closed trainers
- Thin gloves, buff, beanie and sunglasses retainer

### Action daypack
- 20–25L pack and dry pouches
- 1L water plus trail snacks
- Blister kit and personal medication
- Portable charger and Type-F adaptor
- Physical ID and insurance certificate
- All Vy, Entur and operator tickets offline

### Conditional water only
- Swimsuit and microfibre towel
- Spare dry underwear and socks
- No cotton under a supplied wetsuit
- Written weak-swimmer approval saved offline
- Operators supply harness, helmet, wetsuit and technical kit

### Important: Weak-swimmer email
Use: ‘I am a weak swimmer but comfortable in a lifejacket. Does your insurance/risk assessment permit me on this exact activity? Please confirm whether swimming, water entry or self-rescue may be required.’ Never overstate ability; an unclear answer means use the dry plan.

### Important: Red-list water products
Class III/IV rafting, tandem river kayaking, canyoning, SUP and riverboarding are excluded: their published requirements or capsize environment call for confident/strong swimming. Do not swap them into this plan.
Links: [Class IV requirements](https://www.fjordnorway.com/en/see-and-do/class-4-white-water-rafting-with-voss-active-oeh2lojgrvcgnsp1sq5cpq) · [Canyoning product](https://www.vossactive.no/juvvandring/)

### Important: Why Loen is absent
Voss→Loen plus Loen→Bergen costs roughly 13 hours and reduces the air reserve. Ragnarok is expert F-grade, not a safe default. The Classic route is spectacular, but it is the wrong trade for this 11-day, no-car plan.
Links: [Loen route grades](https://en.loenskylift.no/viaferrataloen)

### Important: Budget stop line
£ prices use a conservative £1=NOK12.8 planning conversion; NOK operator prices control. Once booked ground spend reaches £4,250 pp, add no paid fallback and preserve the £750 contingency.
