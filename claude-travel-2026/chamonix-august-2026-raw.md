## Packing and safety

### Alpine daypack · 25–30L
- Waterproof shell and trousers
- Light insulated jacket, fleece, warm gloves and beanie
- Category 4 glacier sunglasses, SPF50 and lip protection
- 1.5–2L water, lunch and high-energy snacks
- Headlamp, blister kit, medication and power bank
- Physical photo ID for the Italian ridge day

### Reserve locally
- Rigid crampon-compatible mountaineering boots
- Compatible crampons and ice axe
- Harness and helmet
- Via-ferrata lanyard if not supplied
- Climbing shoes
- Trekking poles if desired

### Everyday + conditional water
- Closed trainers and hiking boots
- Thin climbing / via-ferrata gloves
- Technical layers and two pairs of hiking socks
- Only if rafting is approved: swimsuit, quick-dry towel and spare shoes
- Offline vouchers, insurance number and guide contacts
- Two payment cards plus some euros

### Important: Weak-swimmer rule
Book no water activity unless the operator confirms in writing that the exact route and expected level require no independent swimming, jumps, submersion, underwater obstacle, capsize drill or unassisted boat re-entry, and a correctly fitted buoyancy aid is worn throughout. An unclear answer means keep the dry hike.

### Important: Strong-swimmer products are excluded
Hydrospeed, Morge Sporty Canyon and Sport Rafting are not part of this itinerary. Morge explicitly requires a 25m swim and underwater-obstacle passage; do not substitute them for the approved plan.
Links: [Hydrospeed requirements](https://www.chamonix-guides.com/fr/activites/details/hydrospeed) · [Morge requirements](https://www.chamonix-guides.com/en/activities/details/canyoning-sporty)

### Important: Group minimums can move the price
Several €83–€370 guide rates require four people. Ask to merge with another departure first. The £500 contingency is protected for a modest private surcharge; if the cap is threatened, drop e-MTB before cutting insurance or guide safety.

### Important: No unguided glacier or ridge improvisation
The guide can change or cancel any objective for wind, lightning, rockfall, lift or participant condition. A cancelled commercial booking is not permission to attempt the route yourselves.
